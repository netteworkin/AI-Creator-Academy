# Prompt Engineering Glossary

## Prompt

Instructions given to an AI model to produce a desired response.

---

## Prompt Engineering

The practice of designing prompts that consistently generate accurate, useful, and well-structured AI outputs.

---

## Role

The perspective or expertise assigned to the AI.

---

## Context

Background information that helps the AI understand the situation.

---

## Objective

The specific task the AI should complete.

---

## Constraints

Rules or limitations that guide the response.

---

## Output Format

The required structure of the AI's response.

---

## Tone

The writing style or personality of the response.

---

## Prompt Library

A curated collection of reusable prompts organized by category or purpose.

---

## Prompt Template

A reusable framework for creating consistent prompts.

---

## Reusable Prompt

A prompt designed to work across multiple scenarios with minimal changes.
