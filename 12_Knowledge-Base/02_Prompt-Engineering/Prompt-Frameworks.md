# Prompt Frameworks

## Purpose

Prompt frameworks provide repeatable structures for creating clear, consistent, and effective AI prompts.

## Frameworks

### RTF
Role • Task • Format

Best for:
- Everyday prompting
- Content creation
- Business writing

---

### CRISPE

Capacity • Role • Insight • Statement • Personality • Experiment

Best for:
- Business strategy
- Marketing
- Planning

---

### Few-Shot Prompting

Teach AI using examples.

Best for:
- Templates
- Standardized outputs
- Repetitive tasks

---

### Persona Prompting

Assign AI an expert identity.

Best for:
- Consulting
- Coaching
- Education

---

### Chain-of-Thought Prompting

Break complex problems into logical steps.

Best for:
- Planning
- Analysis
- Research
- Decision-making
