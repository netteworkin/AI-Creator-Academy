# The A.C.T.I.O.N. Method™

Every AI Creator Academy lesson follows a consistent learning process.

## A — Acquire

Learn the concept.

---

## C — Comprehend

Understand why it matters.

---

## T — Try

Practice through guided exercises.

---

## I — Improve

Refine your work using feedback and iteration.

---

## O — Optimize

Develop reusable systems and best practices.

---

## N — Navigate

Apply your new skills confidently to real-world projects.

---

The goal of AI Creator Academy is not simply to teach AI—it is to help learners confidently apply AI to create meaningful outcomes.
