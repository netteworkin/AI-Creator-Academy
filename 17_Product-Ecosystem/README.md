# AI Creator Academy Product Ecosystem

## Purpose

The Product Ecosystem documents how every idea, prompt, product, and course connects throughout the AI Creator Academy.

It serves as the master planning system for creating, organizing, marketing, bundling, and expanding digital products.

---

## Objectives

- Organize all product ideas from the source PDF.
- Track development from idea to published product.
- Map relationships between books, workbooks, prompt packs, courses, presentations, and memberships.
- Support scalable product creation without duplicating work.
